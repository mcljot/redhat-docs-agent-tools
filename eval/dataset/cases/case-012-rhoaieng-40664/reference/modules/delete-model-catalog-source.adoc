= Delete a model catalog source

[role="_abstract"]
You can delete a previously added catalog source to remove all of its models from the model catalog.

[NOTE]
====
You cannot delete the default catalog sources.
====

.Prerequisites

* You have logged in to the {productname-short} dashboard as an administrator.

* You have added a model catalog source.

.Procedure

. In the {productname-short} dashboard, click *Settings* -> *Model resources and operations* -> *Model catalog settings*.

. In the list of sources, find the source that you want to delete.

. Click the action menu (&#8942;) for the catalog source, and then click *Delete*.

. In the *Delete a source* dialog, enter the source name to confirm the deletion. 

. Click *Delete* to confirm the deletion.

.Verification

. On the *Model catalog settings* page, verify that the deleted catalog source no longer displays in the list.

. Click *AI hub* -> *Catalog*.

. Verify that models from the deleted catalog source are no longer available in the catalog.
